Setup Guide
  1. Open your Google Sheet
  2. Go to Extensions → Apps Script
  3. Delete existing code, paste from PASTE_IN_GOOGLE_SHEET.js
  4. Click Deploy → New deployment
  5. Type: Web app
  6. Execute as: Me
  7. Who has access: Anyone
  8. Click Deploy, authorize, copy the URL
  9. Paste the URL above and click Save!
💡 Use [Name] in templates — auto-fills with lead's first name
